Slab tariffs

Small overruns get expensive.

Household tariffs in Pakistan are charged in slabs. Going over 200 units in a month can move a protected household onto a higher rate.

Five jobs for AI

The numbers come from real data and fixed maths. AI senses, reads, explains and advises.

1

Wi-Fi presence

ESP32 + phone app

Turns Wi-Fi signal wobble into empty, sitting or walking, using a threshold learned for your room.

2

Bill scanner

Gemini vision

Reads a photo of the bill and fills in the 12-month history in about 7 seconds.

3

AI assistant

Gemini, grounded

Answers questions about your bill in English, Urdu or Roman Urdu, from your own data only.

4

Month-end plan

Our maths + Gemini

Our code works out the waste as a range. Gemini explains it and writes a 3 to 5 step plan.

5

Solar advisor

Simulation + Gemini

Simulates 8,760 hours of real sunlight for your location. Gemini recommends one plan.

Keeping the AI honest

AI never invents a number.

  1. RangesWaste, savings and payback are shown as ranges and called estimates.
  2. GroundedThe assistant only sees your bills, slab status, forecast, appliances and waste log.
  3. CheckedBill rows are validated. Months in the future are rejected.
  4. PrivateNo camera or microphone. The bill photo is deleted and account numbers are never read.
1. A real bill scan

The month is taken from the bill's history table, not the due date. Credit bills from solar net-metering are kept, not thrown away.
