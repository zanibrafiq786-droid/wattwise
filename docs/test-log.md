# Test Log

Per `CLAUDE.md`'s rule: test with at least 3 real people, record exactly what they said and what
was changed because of it. **Nothing below is filled in yet -- it needs real people actually
using the app**, not invented quotes. Copy the template for each tester as you go.

## How to run a test session

1. Hand the phone (with Expo Go + the app open) to the person, or install the APK once built.
2. Give them a real task, not a tour: e.g. "check if your room is empty right now," "see if
   anything's been left on," "find out what wasted the most electricity this month."
3. Watch and write down their exact words when they're confused, surprised, or say something
   works well -- not your paraphrase of what they meant.
4. Change one thing based on what you saw, note it below, and (if there's time) have them try the
   changed version.
